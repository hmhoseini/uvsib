Electrochemical Reaction Pathways

Every elementary-step pathway defined across the seven reaction-specific workchains — HER, OER, ORR, CER, CO2RR, NOXRR, NRR — with the free-energy convention and equilibrium potential each one is scored against.

Model. All seven reactions use the computational hydrogen electrode (CHE): each elementary step is written as one dict of stoichiometric coefficients — products positive, reactants negative — and its ΔG is summed directly from DFT/ML electronic energies plus ZPE corrections, never differenced against a neighboring step. A consumed (H⁺ + e⁻) is booked against ½ H₂; CER, referenced to the Cl⁻/Cl₂ couple, books it against ½ Cl₂ instead. Overpotential is the largest step ΔG minus the reaction's equilibrium potential Ueq.

OER Oxygen Evolution Reaction oer.py

4-electron acidic mechanism: 2 H₂O → O₂ + 4H⁺ + 4e⁻. Only one pathway is implemented — the *OH → *O → *OOH adsorbate-evolution route. The final step is not computed directly; it closes the loop against the fixed total ΔG (4 × 1.23 eV), sidestepping DFT's O₂ overbinding error.

4e_mechanism 4 e⁻ Ueq 1.23 V
StepElementary reactionDescription
1H₂O + * → *OH + H⁺ + e⁻Water dissociatively adsorbs, leaving a hydroxyl on the surface.
2*OH → *O + H⁺ + e⁻The adsorbed hydroxyl is deprotonated to an oxo species.
3H₂O + *O → *OOH + H⁺ + e⁻A second water attacks the oxo site, forming a hydroperoxo intermediate.
4*OOH → O₂(g) + * + H⁺ + e⁻O₂ is released and the site regenerated; ΔG set by energy-conserving closure, not computed from a DFT O₂ reference.
